SDLC Methods

1. Agile

Agile is a flexible and iterative approach that focuses on delivering small, functional increments of a project. It emphasizes collaboration, adaptability, and continuous feedback.

  • Iterative development in short cycles (sprints).
  • Frequent delivery of functional software.
  • Close collaboration with stakeholders and team members.
  • Welcomes changing requirements even in later stages.

Best For: Dynamic and evolving project environments where flexibility is essential.

2. Waterfall

Waterfall is a traditional, linear, and sequential approach where each phase of the project is completed before moving to the next.

  • Clearly defined phases: Requirements, Design, Implementation, Testing, Deployment, and Maintenance.
  • Progress flows in one direction like a waterfall.
  • Extensive documentation at every stage.

Best For: Projects with well-defined and stable requirements that are unlikely to change.

3. Scrum

Scrum is a subset of Agile that emphasizes collaboration, accountability, and iterative progress. It uses short development cycles called sprints.

  • Organized in sprints (time-boxed development cycles).
  • Daily stand-up meetings for updates and issue tracking.
  • Roles: Scrum Master, Product Owner, and Development Team.

Best For: Teams that thrive on collaboration and need frequent iterations.

4. Kanban

Kanban is a visual workflow management method that helps teams track tasks and improve process efficiency.

  • Uses boards to visualize work and progress.
  • Limits work in progress to avoid overloading the team.
  • Focuses on continuous delivery without overburdening the team.

Best For: Teams looking to optimize workflows and manage tasks effectively.

5. Lean

Lean minimizes waste and maximizes value delivery by focusing on only the essential project elements.

  • Eliminates non-essential processes and focuses on value.
  • Encourages team empowerment and decision-making.
  • Delivers high-quality results with minimal resources.

Best For: Projects aiming to deliver high value while minimizing waste.
